Expense Management

Employee claims, requests, advances, payments, petty cash, budgets, vendors, reporting, and audit for Odoo 17 Community.

Role-aware workspace

Employees submit expenses, managers approve work, finance completes settlement and accounting, and administrators control policy and access.

Community accounting

Expense events post through standard Odoo accounts, journals, and journal entries. No separate ledger is maintained.

Controlled operations

Multi-company rules, approval routing, fiscal cut-offs, immutable audit events, and server-side authorization protect each workflow.
