Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CleonHR pricing and plans

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. We prioritize security globally across all calculations and configurations. Our platform is built on enterprise-grade cloud servers with SOC 2 compliance, regular external audits, and continuous monitoring. We offer high-fidelity database backing layers, custom storage options for your organizations, and strict zero-data leakage parameters without validation access.

What types of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and direct corporate wire deposits tailored to local deployment regions.

Does CleonHR support multiple languages?

Yes, the platform interface configuration can be toggled across standard multi-language locales for global team coverage.

How flexible are your contracts?

You can choose month-to-month cycles or annual commitments. There are no locking provisions beyond your active cycle parameters.
