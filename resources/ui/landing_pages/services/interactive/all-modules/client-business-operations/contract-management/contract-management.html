Contract Management

Full contract lifecycle management from creation to renewal and expiry

Create, store, track, and manage every employment and vendor contract in a centralized digital repository — with automated alerts, e-signature integration, version control, and complete audit history to protect your organization at every stage.

Start now, it's free

Who It's For

HR & Legal Teams

Draft, negotiate, and execute employee contracts with version control and e-signature support.

Procurement & Finance

Manage vendor contracts, track payment milestones, and receive renewal notifications automatically.

Senior Management

Review contract obligations, monitor upcoming renewals, and ensure compliance with commitments.

Key Features

Digital Contract Repository

Centralized, searchable storage for all contracts with tagging, categorization, and version history.

Contract Templates & Signatures

Pre-built templates for employment letters, NDAs, and service agreements with integrated e-signature capability.

Renewal & Expiry Alerts

Automated notifications sent to owners and approvers before contracts expire or reach renewal windows.

Obligation Tracking

Record contract milestones, payment terms, and deliverables with status tracking and reminder workflows.

How It Helps Your Business

Prevents Contract Lapses

Automated renewal alerts ensure no contract lapses unnoticed, protecting the organization from contractual gaps.

Reduces Legal Risk

Standardized templates and version control prevent unauthorized contract modifications or use of outdated terms.

Speeds Up Contracting

E-signature integration eliminates printing, scanning, and mailing — cutting contract turnaround from days to hours.

Improves Negotiation Visibility

Complete version history shows every change made during negotiation for transparent review and dispute resolution.

Additional Benefits

Centralized contract repository with full-text search

Employment Contract Digitization

An organization with 800 employees migrated all paper employment contracts to the digital repository in 4 weeks, enabling HR to locate any contract in seconds and eliminating the risk of lost documentation.
