Audit Trail

A complete, tamper-proof log of every action across your HR platform

Capture every user action, data change, approval, and system event in an immutable audit trail, giving compliance teams, auditors, and management full visibility into who did what, when, and why across the entire CleonHR ecosystem.

Who It's For

Compliance & Internal Audit Teams

Review change histories, export system activities, and generate compliance reports for regulators.

IT & Security Administrators

Monitor user access patterns, detect anomalies, and investigate unauthorized data access.

Senior Management

Ensure accountability across the organization with complete audit trails for high-risk actions.

Key Features

Comprehensive Activity Logging

Every login, record creation, update, deletion, approval, and export is captured with full context.

Immutable Log Storage

Logs are write-once and tamper-proof, ensuring no user—not even high-level administrators—can alter historical records.

Advanced Search & Filtering

Filter logs by user, module, date range, action type, or data fields to quickly locate specific events.

Compliance Reporting

Generate formatted audit reports for regulatory submissions, internal reviews, or external audits in one click.

How It Helps Your Business

Supports Regulatory Compliance

Meet data protection, labor laws, and financial reporting requirements with verifiable activity records.

Enables Incident Investigation

Trace any data breach, unauthorized change, or system anomaly back to the source quickly and accurately.

Deters Fraudulent Activity

Knowing all actions are logged creates a culture of accountability and discourages misconduct.

Reduces Audit Preparation Time

Auditors can self-serve logs directly, eliminating weeks of manual data extraction by IT teams.
