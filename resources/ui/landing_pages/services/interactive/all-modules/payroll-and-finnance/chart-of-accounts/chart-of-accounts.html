Chart of Accounts

A structured financial account hierarchy that powers your entire HR finance ecosystem

Define, organize, and maintain your company's complete chart of accounts — mapping every payroll cost, HR expense, and benefit into the right account hierarchy so your financial reports are always accurate, compliant, and audit-ready.

Start now, it's free

Who It's For

Finance & Accounting Teams

Build and maintain a robust chart structure optimized for financial reporting and ERP integration.

HR Finance Managers

Ensure payroll, costs, benefits, and statutory liabilities clear to the correct GL accounts seamlessly.

External Auditors

Review a clear, well-structured account hierarchy with full transaction trace visibility.

Key Features

Hierarchical Account Structure

Create multi-level account groups (Assets, Liabilities, Income, Expenses) with unlimited sub-accounts.

Payroll Account Mapping

Map payroll components (basic salary, allowances, deductions, taxes) to specific GL accounts automatically.

Cost Center Integration

Link accounts to cost centers for departmental financial reporting and precise cost allocation.

Import & Export

Import existing charts from Excel or ERP systems and export for external audit or compliance reporting.

How It Helps Your Business

Ensures Reporting Accuracy

Correctly mapped accounts minimize bookkeeping errors, redundant statements, and recurring audit corrections.

Simplifies Audit Preparation

A well-structured CoA with clear account definitions makes financial audits smoother and shorter.

Supports Compliance

Align your account structure to IFRS, GAAP, or local statutory reporting requirements.

Powers Automated Journal Entries

Payroll processing auto-generates journal entries to the correct accounts, eliminating manual posting.

Additional Benefits

  • Unlimited account code flexibility and nesting
  • Automatic payroll-to-GL account mapping
  • Integration with Journal Entries and payroll modules
  • Pre-built templates aligned to standard accounting frameworks
  • Version history and change audit trail
  • Export to CSV, Excel, or ERP-compatible formats

Real-World Use Cases

Payroll-to-GL Automation

A manufacturing company mapped 42 major expense points to their Chart of Accounts, enabling payroll to auto-post to the correct GL codes. Month-end accounting operations that previously took 2 days now complete in under ten minutes.

Audit Compliance Overhaul

A mid-sized firm restructured its chart of accounts to align with IFRS standards using the import tool, passing an external audit with zero reclassification adjustments for the first time.
