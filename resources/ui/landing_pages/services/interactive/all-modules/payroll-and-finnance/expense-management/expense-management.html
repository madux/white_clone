Expense Management

Effortless employee expense tracking, approval, and reimbursement

Simplify the entire expense lifecycle — from receipt capture and claim submission to multi-level approval and reimbursement processing — with a mobile-first platform that enforces policy compliance automatically.

Start now, it's free

Who It's For

Employees

Submit expense claims on the go, attach receipts, and track reimbursement status in real-time.

Finance & Accounts Teams

Review, approve, and process expense reimbursements with full audit visibility.

Department Managers

Approve faster requests, achieve spend control, and monitor department expense budgets.

Key Features

Mobile Receipt Capture

Snap and upload receipts instantly from the mobile app, with auto-extract amount, merchant, and date via OCR.

Policy Enforcement

Configure spend limits and statutory rules, automatically flagging out-of-compliance claims.

Multi-Level Approval Workflow

Route expense claims through line managers and finance approvers with automated workflows.

Reimbursement Processing

Safely process approved reimbursements directly to payroll or bank accounts.

How It Helps Your Business

Accelerates Reimbursements

Reduce average reimbursement cycles from 2-3 weeks to 3-5 business days with automated routing.

Reduces Policy Violations

Automatic policy checks catch out-of-policy expenses before they reach finance, cutting violations by up to 70%.

Improves Visibility

Real-time dashboards show pending claims, approved amounts, and budget consumption by category.

Eliminates Paper Receipts

Digital receipt storage means no lost receipts and easy retrieval during audits.

Additional Benefits

OCR-powered receipt scanning to eliminate manual entry
Expense category rules aligned to company policy
Integration with payroll for seamless reimbursement
Real-time status update tracking notifications
Multi-currency expense submission for international travel
Exportable expense reports for tax and audit purposes
