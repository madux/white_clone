Petty Cash

Complete control over petty cash funds, disbursements, and reconciliation

Manage petty cash across all departments and locations with full transparency — from fund replenishment requests and disbursement tracking to receipt matching, custodian accountability, and automated month-end reconciliation.

Start now, it's free

Who It's For

Finance & Accounts Teams

Monitor petty cash balances, review disbursements, and reconcile fund usage across departments.

Petty Cash Custodians

Record disbursements, attach receipts, and request fund replenishment with a simple digital workflow.

Department Managers & Auditors

Approve expenditures, review transaction history, and ensure policy compliance across all funds.

Key Features

Fund Setup & Custodian Management

Create multiple petty cash funds by department or location, assign custodians, and set spending limits and categories.

Disbursement Recording

Log every cash payment with purpose, category, receipt attachments, and approval in real-time from any device.

Replenishment Workflow

Custodians submit replenishment requests when the fund falls below the threshold; finance approves and records the top-up digitally.

Reconciliation & Reporting

Automated period-end reconciliation comparing physical cash balance against recorded disbursements, with downloadable reports for audit.

How It Helps Your Business

Eliminates Cash Leakage

Every disbursement is logged and receipt-matched, closing the gaps that allow undocumented spending and petty pilferage.

Simplifies Reconciliation

Automated running balance and end-of-period reconciliation reports replace manual spreadsheet tallies, saving hours every month.

Improves Audit Readiness

A complete, searchable transaction trail with attached receipts means petty cash audits are completed in minutes, not hours.

Enforces Spending Policies

Category limits and approval workflows prevent unauthorized spending before disbursements are made, not after the fact.

Additional Benefits

  • Support for multiple petty cash funds across locations
  • Mobile receipt capture with automatic balance update
  • Integration with Chart of Accounts for automatic journal posting
  • Configurable per-transaction and daily spending limits
  • Role-based access for custodians, approvers, and auditors
  • Trend reports showing spend patterns by category and department

Real-World Use Cases

Multi-Branch Retail Chain

A retail chain with 20 branches replaced paper-based petty cash registers with digital funds per location. Monthly reconciliation time dropped from 2 hours per branch to under 15 minutes, and unaccounted disbursements fell to zero within the first quarter.

NGO Field Operations

A non-profit managing field programs across 6 states gave each state coordinator a capped petty cash fund. Real-time visibility into balances and spending enabled the finance team to spot a reporting anomaly and prevent a ₦450,000 loss.
