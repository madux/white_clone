Global Employee Management System (GEMS)

Unified workforce management across multiple countries, entities, and currencies

Manage a geographically distributed workforce from a single platform — with country-specific compliance, multi-currency payroll, localized HR policies, and consolidated global reporting so you can scale internationally without losing operational control.

Start now, it's free

Who It's For

Multinational Corporations & Enterprises

Centralize HR operations across a wide cluster of multiple countries without matching local compliance.

Global HR Leaders

Access consolidated workforces analytics and look out for localized global policies from a single dashboard.

Local HR & Payroll Teams

Operate within a globally standardized system while adhering to country-specific regulatory tax requirements.

Key Features

Multi-Country Configuration

Set up country-specific workflow rules, tax structures, leave policies, and statutory compliance requirements dynamically.

Multi-Currency Payroll

Process payroll in multiple currencies with real-time exchange rates and consolidated reporting in your group base currency.

Global Headcount Dashboard

Unified view of total workforce across all international legal entities filterable by entity, department, and employment type.

Cross-Entity Transfers

Manage international assignments, intra-company transfers, with variable packages within the same system.

How It Helps Your Business

Eliminates Fragmented Systems

Replace multiple country-specific HR platforms with one integrated platform, reducing IT costs and data silos significantly.

Ensures Local Compliance

Country-specific compliance filters are monitored and updated within the platform, reducing the risk of statutory violations abroad.

Enables Global Workforce Visibility

Executive access to consolidated headcount, cost, and talent analytics models across all geographies in real-time.

Simplifies International Mobility

Expatriate management, host country payroll, and shadow payroll handling within a single workflow.

Additional Benefits

Support for 50+ country compliance frameworks
Multi-currency payroll with automatic FX conversion
Consolidated global HR reports in the group currency
Entity-level data controls for regional privacy compliance
Centralized policy management with local overrides
Single sign-on across all global business branches

Real-World Use Cases

Pan-African Expansion

A financial services group expanded across Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa unified HR operations on GEMS, eliminating 4 separate payroll systems and running consolidated group HR reporting for the first time — reducing close time from 2 weeks to 2 days.

Expatriate Assignment Management

A multinational managed 45 expatriate assignments across 12 countries using GEMS, automating host country payroll, cost equalization computations, and shadow payroll — cutting the time spent on expat administration by 70%.
