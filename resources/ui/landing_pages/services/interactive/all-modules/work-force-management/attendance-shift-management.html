Attendance & Shift Management

Accurate clock-in/out and intelligent shift scheduling in one platform

Manage employee attendance, shift patterns, and scheduling with precision — from biometric clock-ins to automated shift rotation, ensuring workforce availability aligns with operational demand at all times.

Start now, it's free

Who It's For

Operations Managers

Build and publish shift schedules, manage coverage gaps, and handle last-minute changes.

HR Administrators

Monitor attendance trends, enforce punctuality policies, and generate compliance reports.

Employees

View assigned shifts, swap shifts, and clock in/out via mobile or book.

Key Features

Flexible Clock-In Methods

Support biometric, mobile GPS, QR code, and web-based clock-in options.

Shift Scheduler

Drag-and-drop shift builder with conflict detection and automated rotation patterns.

Overtime & Exception Tracking

Automatically flag tardiness, absences, and overtime for manager review.

Real-Time Attendance Dashboard

Live view of who is clocked in, on break, or absent across departments.

How It Helps Your Business

Eliminates Buddy Punching

Biometric and GPS-verified clock-ins ensure only the assigned employee can mark attendance.

Reduces Scheduling Gaps

AI-assisted scheduling prevents under-staffing by analyzing historical attendance patterns.

Streamlines Payroll Integration

Attendance data flows directly to payroll, eliminating manual timesheet reconciliation.

Improves Accountability

Transparent attendance records reduce disputes and build a culture of punctuality.

Additional Benefits

Geofenced mobile clock-ins to prevent remote fraud
Automated shift reminders via push notifications
Shift swap and cover requests with manager approval
Customizable work schedules (9-hour, compressed, flexi)
Integrated with payroll for seamless wage calculation
Historical attendance analytics and trend reports

Real-World Use Cases

Multi-Site Retail Management

A retail chain managing 45 stores used Attendance & Shift Management to enforce GPS-verified clock-ins, reducing time theft and saving an estimated $18k monthly in unearned wages.
